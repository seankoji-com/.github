# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss the work, not the person.
Welcome questions asked in good faith, make room for different experience
levels, and give people a reasonable chance to correct mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, and
sexualized attention or imagery.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to an organization owner through GitHub.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban an
account when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as
privately as the circumstances allow.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone represents a
`seankoji-com` project in public.
